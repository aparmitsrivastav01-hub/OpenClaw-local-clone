## Key Audit Areas & Recommendations

| Audit Area | Why It Matters | Recommended Auditing Steps |
|-----------|----------------|---------------------------|
| **Retail Asset Valuation** | 5 % of total revenue is retail; high inventory and real‑estate assets need accurate valuation.| • Inspect physical inventory counts, leasehold properties. <br>• Verify impairment testing per IAS 36 using own cash‑flow models. |
| **Commodity Hedging & Derivatives** | Hedging mitigates price risk but introduces valuation complexity.| • Recalculate hedge effectiveness via regression tests. <br>• Confirm hedge documentation, cash‑flow projections with finance team. |
| **Revenue from Integrated Services (Infrastructure)** | Multi‑country operations with contractual revenue in different currencies.| • Test foreign‑currency translation and allowance for uncollectible accounts. |
| **Intangible Asset Accounting** | Large R&D expenditure on telecom 5G stack.| • Verify amortization schedule vs license agreements. <br>• Perform impairment review under IAS 36. |
| **Taxation – Transfer Pricing** | Cross‑border transactions with subsidiary hub in UAE.| • Inspect transfer‑price documentation, PE risk analysis. |
| **Environmental, Social & Governance (ESG)** | Impact on future materiality and potential regulatory scrutiny.| • Cross‑check disclosed ESG metrics with external sustainability reports. |
